Delete your account or data

For apps by Tareq Islam on Google Play

How to ask

Send an email to mti.tareq3@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Include:

  • The app name
  • The email or user ID you used in the app

What gets deleted

Your account and all personal data linked to it.

What may be kept

Data the law requires me to keep, such as payment records. It is kept only as long as needed, then deleted.

How long it takes

Within 30 days of your request. You will get a reply by email when it is done.
